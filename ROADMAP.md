# Roads of the North: upgrade loop

Each round: build one batch, test it (Node sims in /home/claude/tests + headless phone screenshots), push here
(GitHub builds the APK), and update the web link. Rules: real Skyrim numbers where they exist ([Skyrim]),
our own marked [OURS]; plain-language UI; phone first.

## Player reports
Player reports: GitHub issues labelled player-report; Claude picks them up and fixes them.
- In the game: F12 on a PC, or Settings → Report a problem on a phone. The kind (bug, balance, art, text, idea) is a second label.
- The server (server/src/reports.js) needs the env var GITHUB_REPORT_TOKEN on Railway: a fine-grained token for R3MC10/Skyrim-Roads with Issues: read and write. Without it reports are kept in the server's database (reports table) and the log says "report stored (no token)".
- Limits: 5 reports an hour per IP, 20 KB description, 300 KB whole report. Offline reports wait on the device and send themselves later.
- Stored reports can be read back with `REPORTS_READ_KEY=… node scripts/reports.mjs` once the owner sets the env var REPORTS_READ_KEY on Railway (a long random secret; unset = the read routes are off).

## Standing rules
- Perk trees grow with the game: every new system (gathering, magic, encounters, crafting, party play...) adds or adjusts perks with real effects in the matching trees (perkfx.js), keeping old perk ids and a clean star layout.
- Party size up to 8 players.
- No player clans or guilds any more: at level 20 players pick a side (Stormcloaks, Imperial Legion, Aldmeri Dominion). Holds, war points and Jarls belong to sides. Attacking another player is always a choice (with a confirm) and never allowed on players under 20, without a side, in your party, in a dungeon or just knocked down. Cross-side losers are knocked down and keep their gear; bounty still applies. Side cloaks: every effect and power belongs to one side only (test_sides.js checks it).
- One art style: hand-drawn (pixel art was removed on 24 Nov 2026).
- In a party, dungeon choices (which way, what to do in a room) are voted by everyone in the run: faces by the picks, majority wins, a tie goes the leader's way, 20 s timer (partyvote.js). On the road the leader still decides and the others' votes are advice. Loot is voted at the end of a dungeon, gold split evenly.
